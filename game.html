VIBEGANG

Seven gangs run the VibeVibe testnet streets. Pick yours, build your crew, take their turf, keep it.

3D graphics aren't available in this browser. Try Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

Pick your gang

You become their face. Your crew wears their colors. The other six run the rest of the city, and they want it all.

How to play

🎯 Your goalTake over the city block by block. Every block you own pays you every minute, and more blocks pay more.
🟩 The colorsThe ground shows who owns each block. Green is yours. Every rival gang has its own color, and grey means nobody. Press T for the full map.
⚔️ Take a blockWalk into an enemy block and take out 3 of their members. A gang war starts: beat 3 waves and the block is yours.
🚨 Keep itGangs come back. When the red alarm says your turf is under attack, follow the arrow and fight them off.
📦 FortifyPick up supply crates (or buy them at Guild HQ). Stand in your block and press G for barricades, guards and a turret.
⭐ Level upWins earn respect. Higher levels unlock better guns, a tank and a helicopter at the VIBE AMMO shop.
👮 CopsThey only come if a cop sees you commit a crime. The banner tells you why. Lose them and the stars fade.
🎮 ControlsWASD move, mouse aims, click shoots, E get in or out and enter places, 1-7 weapons, H medkit, T map, G fortify, C crew. Phone: left stick moves, right stick aims and shoots.
