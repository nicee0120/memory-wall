The hustle

Four moves to run the city. The game walks you through each one.

  1. Take their turfWalk into a rival block and drop three of them. A gang war starts: beat three waves and the block is yours, painted green.
  2. Keep itThey come back. When the alarm goes off, follow the red arrow and push them out before the clock runs out.
  3. FortifyGrab supply crates and medkits around town. Build barricades, post guards and set up a turret on your blocks.
  4. Level upEvery block pays you every minute. Earn respect for better guns, a bigger crew, a tank and a helicopter.
